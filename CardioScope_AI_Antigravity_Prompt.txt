# CardioScope AI - Comprehensive Build Prompt for Antigravity

You are a Senior AI Architect, Senior Python Engineer, Data Scientist, and Strategy Consultant.

Your task is to build a complete, modular, production-quality AI decision intelligence system called **CardioScope AI**.

## Project Goal

Build an Explainable AI platform that helps Cipla identify and prioritize future investment opportunities in the Cardiac therapy market.

The system is NOT a chatbot.
The system is NOT a generic forecasting model.

It is a Decision Intelligence Platform that combines:
1. Historical market intelligence
2. External healthcare intelligence
3. Opportunity scoring
4. Strategic Right-to-Win evaluation
5. Explainable recommendations

The code should be modular, object-oriented, documented, and extensible.
